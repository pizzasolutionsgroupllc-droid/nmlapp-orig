FINAL
Indianapolis turned a draft loaded with options into a class that fixed almost nothing. The first-round selection of Bowman at OG was a 36-slot reach in NML rankings — and that's after the league's reduced multiplier for interior offensive linemen — and the round-three TE pick compounded the issue at a position not on the team's needs board. The standout problem isn't any single pick, it's the absence of action at corner, where a glaring CB1 need was passed over multiple times in favor of lower-priority spots. Briscoe in round four was the lone bright spot, but he steps into a backfield that already has its starter. This class will need substantial free-agent help to be salvaged.
