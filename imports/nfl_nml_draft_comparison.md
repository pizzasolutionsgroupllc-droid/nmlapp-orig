## 7. Key Findings for Draft Suggestion Engine

**NML consistently overdrafts (reaches for):** 
**NML consistently underdrafts (sleeps on):** OT, DT, G, EDGE, C

**Implication for suggestion engine:**
- When a player at an NML-overdrafted position is available, flag that they may go sooner than NFL ADP suggests
- When a player at an NML-underdrafted position is available, flag them as a potential value who may slip
- Use NML historical round averages (not NFL) as the baseline for "expected round" predictions

**Speed premium reconfirmed:** NML drafts WR and CB at consistently higher rates and earlier rounds vs NFL,
especially for players with 90+ speed ratings — this is the single strongest NML drafting divergence.
