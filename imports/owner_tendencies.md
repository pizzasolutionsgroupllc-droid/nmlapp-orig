## Las Vegas Raiders — Commissioner / Jon Deep Dive
*Jon has been Raiders owner since 2020. Was Chargers (2019) and Bucs (2012-2018). Data below is Raiders-only (2020+).*

### GM Philosophy
Jon does not draft based on school or NFL team loyalty. He operates a distinct asset-accumulation
meta-strategy that differs fundamentally from all other NML owners:

- **Asset accumulation over winning:** Primary goal is maximizing total asset value (players + picks)
  rather than chasing championships each season. Won a title in 2014/15, then pivoted to this approach.
- **Positional surplus drafting:** Deliberately drafts 2-3 players at the same position, develops them,
  then trades the surplus for more value than the original pick cost. Creates net asset gain.
- **Prospect flipping:** Identifies players that other owners will overpay for, acquires them cheap,
  holds ≤2 seasons, then sells high. Known targets: Giants, Panthers, Broncos owners.
- **Redundancy at need positions:** When filling a true roster need, always drafts at least 2 at that
  position (3 for WR) due to bust rate. If he needs a LT, he drafts 2.
- **Indirect fill trades:** Drafts a high-value combo (e.g. fast EDGE + WR) then trades the combo
  for the specific player he actually wants at a different position.
- **Youth maintenance:** Actively maintains one of the league's youngest average rosters.
- **Pick hoarding cycles:** Periodically tears down and restocks with a flood of youth + picks.
- **Proxy drafter:** Frequently acquires picks from owners who dislike drafting, can't attend, or need to leave early. Makes selections on their behalf, then trades most of those acquired players away before the 53-man roster cut. This inflates raw pick counts significantly — total picks-made far exceeds intent-to-keep rate.
- **Trade-back opportunist:** Will trade down from any slot when no compelling player is available or when another team makes an offer too good to decline. Actively accumulates more picks mid-draft this way.

### Current Cycle (2025-26 Season)
- **Goal:** Acquire 16 of 32 picks in the 2026 NML R1 draft (half the league's first round).
- **Price paid:** Traded away Patrick Mahomes, Budda Baker, Devon Achane, AJ Terrell.
- **Result:** Intentionally weak QB (JJ McCarthy 71 OVR), surplus of elite CBs held as trade chips.
- 3 elite CBs on roster (McDuffie 94, Stingley 92, Sauce Gardner 90) = asset surplus, not depth need.

### School Note

- Slight UW lean when two players are otherwise comparable — lives in Seattle area. Not strong enough to override value.
